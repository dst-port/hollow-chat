Community Guidelines

Last updated 30 August 2026 · applies to the official service at hollowchat.org

HollowChat is end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read your servers, your channels, or your direct messages, and we don't want to. That shapes how these rules work: what happens in a shared community — where other members can see it and report it — is held to a fuller standard than a private conversation, where the only thing we ever see is what a participant chooses to send us in a report.

1 · Server communities

Servers, channels, and any space shared with people beyond a one-to-one or small private group.

Communities on HollowChat are spaces you invite others into. Server owners and moderators are responsible for their community, and the following are not allowed anywhere in a server:

  • Hate and harassment — attacking, demeaning, or promoting hatred against people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, disability, or serious disease. Slurs, hateful imagery, and dehumanising "jokes" included.
  • Targeted abuse and bullying — coordinated pile-ons, sustained insults, or following someone across channels to harass them.
  • Violent threats and violent extremism — threatening, encouraging, or glorifying violence, or organising / promoting a violent extremist or terrorist group.
  • Doxxing — posting someone's private or identifying information (real name, address, workplace, phone, documents) without their consent.
  • Sexual content involving minors — see section 3. Zero tolerance, no context makes it acceptable.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, and sexual content in spaces not clearly marked and gated as adult.
  • Spam, scams, phishing, and malware — mass unsolicited messaging, deceptive links, token/crypto scams, malicious files.
  • Illegal goods and services — trading drugs, weapons, stolen accounts or data, forged documents, and similar.
  • Impersonation of a person or organisation in a way meant to deceive.
  • Ban evasion — returning to a community, or to the service, after being removed.

2 · Direct messages & private groups

One-to-one DMs and small invite-only groups. End-to-end encrypted; we never see the contents.

We do not moderate the substance of private conversations — opinions, arguments, dark humour, and things one person finds distasteful are between the people in the chat. But a private channel is not a shield for using another person as a target. The following are always prohibited and can be acted on when reported:

  • Threats of violence or serious harm to a person or their family.
  • Doxxing — collecting or threatening to expose someone's private information to intimidate or coerce them.
  • Blackmail, sextortion, and coercion — demanding money, images, or actions under threat.
  • Sexual content involving minors — see section 3.
  • Sharing intimate images of someone without their consent.
  • Harassment after a clear "stop". If someone tells you to stop contacting them, or blocks you, and you keep messaging them — including sending slurs, racist or hateful abuse, or persistent unwanted messages by other means — that is harassment, and we will act on it even though the messages were private. "It was a DM" is not a defence once the other person has asked you to stop.

3 · Never, anywhere

Regardless of where it happens or who can see it, HollowChat has no tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or the sexualisation of minors in any form. Accounts involved are terminated immediately and reports are escalated to the relevant authorities. The same applies to content that directly furthers a terrorist attack or mass-casualty violence.

4 · Reporting

Report a message from its context menu → Report Message. The report — the reason you write, the messages you attach, and any screenshot — is sealed with encryption to an offline moderator key. Our server stores only who reported whom and when; it cannot read the report itself. A moderator decrypts it on a separate machine, reviews it, and follows up.

For threats to someone's safety, or anything involving a minor, also contact your local law enforcement — we can act on the account but we are not an emergency service.

5 · Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, action may be any of:

  • a warning;
  • removal of the offending content or channel;
  • removal from a specific community;
  • temporary suspension of the account;
  • permanent termination of the account.

CSAM and credible threats of mass violence go straight to termination plus a report to authorities, with no prior warning.

6 · Self-hosted instances

HollowChat's server is open source and anyone can run their own. These guidelines govern the instance we operate at hollowchat.org. An independent instance sets and enforces its own rules; we have no visibility into or control over it.

7 · Changes

We'll update these guidelines as the service grows and post the date of the latest change at the top. Continuing to use HollowChat after a change means you accept the current version.
